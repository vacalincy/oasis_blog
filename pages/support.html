Frequently Asked Questions

How do I format my articles?
OASIS uses a seamless WYSIWYG editor. You can format your text by highlighting it to reveal the floating toolbar, which includes options for bolding, italics, links, and blockquotes. Images can be dragged and dropped directly into the editor body.
Can I schedule a post for later?
Currently, you can save your posts as Drafts to publish them manually when you are ready. Scheduled publishing is on our roadmap and will be released in the next major update.
How do I reset my password?
If you've forgotten your password, click the "Log In" button on the homepage and select "forgotten password?". You will be prompted to enter your email address to receive a secure reset link.
How do I customize my profile?
Navigate to your Dashboard and click on the "Edit Profile" button located beneath your avatar. From there, you can update your display name, bio, and upload a custom header image.

Can't find what you're looking for?

Our support team is available Monday through Friday to help you out.
